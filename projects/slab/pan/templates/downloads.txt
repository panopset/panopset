<h2>Running...</h2>

<h3>Using a platform specific installer.</h3>

<p>
Substitute flywheel, in the <b>Launch path</b> for your platform listed above,
with the Panopset <a href="./product.html">application</a> name of your choice.
</p>

<h3>Using a standalone application.</h3>
<p>
Unzip the standalone application for your platform, cd to the directory that you place the contents in, and then
run the application of your choice as shown in the example for your platform above.
</p>
